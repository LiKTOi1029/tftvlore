granted her no aid, nor the final blow.

he stole the force of eternity,
the power of the gods. it is the opportunity
to turn powerful and prosper by yourself,
and in the hands of man such power soon shall perish!

Laun wanted to find out what power
this force has over the lands of the Valley.
he knew not what the deities could perform.
the very thought of this  alone makes our hands shake!

in his journey to the Valley, he met
the heir of Teisumas, Jega, which had fallen -
when the daughter and son of Teisumas consummated,
their souls both perished fiercely.

Jega was powerful, and boundlessly so  -
his strength splits walls into two!
the dreadful Laun went to seek knowledge for himself,
he rubbed his hands and thought of something -
he shall seek knowledge alongside the seed of Teisumas,
to find out what even the ghost of Narsa knew not.

the monster Laun forced his way to Gudriba,
and demanded her bounty of knowledge.
and when Gudriba refused to surrender,
Laun decided to free him from his body -
to pieces they tore the great god,
his remnants scattered in the meadow.
Jega their bones ground into a porridge,
and Laun furiously hissed.

the world is upside down and darkened,
and we know not what now it stands...
the gods have fallen, in the Valley we remain,

A Tattered Diary, 43 AG
(Official translation by Naktigonis, original in Lithuanian)

THE FIRST DAY - THURSDAY - FOURTH OF LEAF FALL [NOVEMBER], THREE AND FORTY YEARS AFTER THE GODS

We, the Six, a Brave company of the Valley's kin, tired of slavery to uncertainty, exhausted from the vigilant glares of death upon our souls, longing for the pure grace and love of the Gods, dared assemble for a singular purpose - to destroy the Unknown, force its restless waves to ebb, and, with providence of the Gods, to restore order in the great wide world. May Narsa, her name be blessed, light us the way towards victory.

On a Thursday we six, masking ourselves with names not given to us at birth, began our difficult work, which will demand its toll of time. We - Artminas (Nearmention), Girgaudas (Groverumble), Sarmateia, Baltvydis (Whitesight), Tolgrindis (Farpave) and Me, Yoras, - abandoned our native homes, supplied with patience and also weapons. We took everything with us in small parcels - water in mere flasks, bread in singular slices, and Baltvydis, an old greyhaired friend, also brought some herb brew in a jug - allegedly it "shrinks wounds well". He is a man of simple, yet true words, thus I trust him. We wished not to encumber ourselves much, as our journey is to be kept secret. By rattling through the Valley covered in all sorts of supplies we'll only attract attention - and therein are such scum that worship the Unknown. Narsa is turning in its grave! But should they attack us, the bastards, we'll fight 'til the last drop of blood. Thus we begin our journey... 

_________________________________________

FOURTH DAY - SUNDAY - SEVENTH OF LEAF FALL, THREE AND FORTY YEARS AFTER THE GODS

We, the Six, a Brave company of the Valley's kin, the blessèd champions of Narsa, have run into our first difficulties on the road. The crazed scum! Before the Shattered Pass, that ice-wrought realm, we were attacked by a band of evildoers, their souls be cursed! We're only lucky that Baltvydis, our wiseman, felt the disaster coming, and noticed the ambush. In the snows these brigands were hiding, and we cut them up with swords and daggers with little trouble, as soon as they attacked us. Let them know whom they're dealing with!

The frost is getting to us singularly. Artminas, disliking such weather, is shivering for the second day. The last several snowstorms did not do him much good - he began coughing hollowly, and the mountain air is rare. Sarmateia gave him some sort of herbs. Perhaps they'll help. She and Baltvydis know much.

In the name of Narsa, we shall journey onwards.

_________________________________________

SEVENTH DAY - WEDNESDAY - TENTH OF LEAF FALL, THREE AND FORTY YEARS AFTER THE GODS

We, the Six, a Brave company of the Valley's kin, feel further concern. Something unholy permeates the air. This has been going on for long now - ever since Narsa vanished and the Gods perished. Baltvydis was still young then! After Godfall, the stench of death has lingered in the air. People began losing their sense and sanity, falling like the autumn leaves...

But now still these curses linger in the air, and fill the lungs. And we are concerned, for the closer we get to our destination, the more the stench of the Unknown curls our noses. At least we know that we are headed towards the right place.

But we shall not surrender to the Unknown. After all, our purpose is to defeat it!
Artminas is not getting better - the cold air of the Crossing must have hit him hard. He shudders and twitches. Sarmateia fears that we'll have to stop. I'd be afraid of that too - the vast Prairie has nowhere to shelter us for now. We'll be like sitting ducks, only to be shot by someone. And we ourselves have no arrows, only daggers and swords.

A long road awaits us in the Prairie. We shall push onwards, and pray to the Gods, may in their afterlife they hear our prayers, to heal Artminas.

In the name of Narsa, we shall journey onwards.

_________________________________________

EIGHTH DAY - THURSDAY - ELEVENTH OF LEAF FALL, THREE AND FORTY YEARS AFTER THE GODS

We, the Six, a Brave company of the Valley's kin, have lost our first warrior. At night, when we stopped at the edges, Artminas, previously having shivered the whole day, went mad in his own bedsheets. He began shaking unlike himself, foaming from the mouth, blood pouring from his eyes, nose and ears... Not Baltvydis, neither Sarmateia could do anything this fast. The occurrence was finished in half a minute.

At midnight we and Naktis, the goddess of the Moon and the Lady of the Dark Skies, hallowed be Her name, mourned our fallen comrade. May they who hear of his sacrifice know that Artminas, by his true name Eigaudas, laid his head for a noble cause. 

Having buried him quietly, we shall onwards march. Sarmateia is starting to feel unwell. Tolgrindis, already having been silent for the entire journey, a marcher and not a talker, is now even more grim. I myself am taken by sorrow - Eigaudas was like a brother to me. Girgaudas keeps murmuring something, his nerve seems to be breaking. I think I saw him twitching. Must've been sobbing... Only Baltvydis remains the most stoic of us all.

Death stalks in the shadows. In the name of Narsa, we shall journey onwards.

_________________________________________

TENTH DAY - SATURDAY - THIRTEENTH OF LEAF FALL, THREE AND FORTY YEARS AFTER THE GODS

We, the Six, a Brave company of the Valley's kin, suffer further. Crossing the Prairie, almost reaching its end, we were attacked - but not by bandits. Servants of the shadows, scum of the Unknown, well-armed attacked us. Cursed darkness! We fought bravely, like true champions of Narsa. But two of us bravely met Death, travelling to the afterlife. Girgaudas, by true name Visgirdas, and Tolgrindis, by true name Tulminas, were claimed by servants of the Unknown.

Their sacrifice will be in our memory forever. May the Gods have mercy on them. Rest in peace.

Of us six, three remain - Me, Baltvydis and Sarmateia. A Brave company of the Valley's kin. We shall spill our blood 'til the Unknown and its servants perish!

In the name of Narsa, we shall journey onwards.

_________________________________________

TWELFTH DAY - WEDNESDAY - SIXTEENTH OF LEAF FALL, THREE AND FORTY YEARS AFTER THE GODS

We, the Six, a Brave company of the Valley's kin... near our destination. It is getting hard to write... to think... Arms are getting heavier, my head feels like it is filled with hot water... The heart beats wrathfully, my veins pulsate...

The weather feels stuffy, though Siel's peninsula is cool... Here the Sea whispers to me dark thoughts... But I must hold fast. For the Valley, and the memory of my comrades...

It seems we all are weakening, save for Baltvydis. He leads the way energetically... but angrily... he glares at us, and he lashed out cursing at Sarmateia once she stumbled... Baltvydis is unwell, though differently than us.

Baltvydis dashed through the small Siel's peninsula... Found some place, like a dog smelling a ham... 'A well', he says...

Sarmateia fears to go onward. I begin to fear as well, but even more, I begin to turn angry... at Baltvydis... Old geezer... It was his idea to travel here... Malevolent bastard...

I'm beginning to hear a hushed/husky voice of a woman from somewhere. It calls for me... It calls for us all...

In the name of Narsa... we shall journey...

_________________________________________

I write in a hurry. We stopped by the 'well' for the night. My strength is as if returned temporarily to me now, perhaps from the nervousness. Sarmateia is gone, only a bloodstain left upon her sheets. Next to that, Baltvydis' dagger. What happened? Were we attacked? I saw how Baltvydis, with messy hair, in torn clothes (now tattered rags) dove into that well. I hear him laughing. I hear a woman's voice. Sarmateia must be there.

My hands shake, my heart beats like crazy, my veins are on fire, my head is splitting in half...

In the name of Narsa... may the Gods help us.

_________________________________________

Our company is doomed. I'm doomed. We are all dead already.

I went down the well. There was a cave down there. Baltvydis stood before a statue with boney fingers. Chucked something over his shoulders. All bloodied, twisted, twitching. Glowing purple. Then I saw her.

Sarmateia. He killed her, that scum... And he laughed the entire time. Though now he began to growl.

I drew my blade. Led by Narsa... He threw Sarmateia aside, and began dashing towards me, screaming something. I stabbed him...

What scum am I! Blinded by a purple rage, I stabbed at his body many times, 'til my blade sunk into his torso to the hilt, and I was covered in his blood. And he was like a real father to me... Sarmateia... Poor Sarmateia...

Let the Valley and the world know, that Baltvydis, by true name Sanginas, having lost his sanity, turning from the Gods, in cold blood murdered Sarmateia, by true name Mirta, a once-loved pure-hearted comrade. Rest in peace, may the Gods have mercy on your souls.

Regaining my senses after the confrontation, I begin to weaken... weaken... I see a silhouette lurking in the shadows... she's covered herself with her scarf mysteriously, turned away... she whispers to me... closer...

the hand... rises towards the dagger... she whispers...

dagger... body. dagger. body. dagger.

Ode to Dwindling Hope
(Original English contribution by Naktigonis)

The world has lost its crownèd head -
Narsa lies slain, her body dead.
Now struggles Laun to fight the dark -
The shadows come, so cold and stark.

Gudriba fell, his powers gave
To Laun, which now becomes a slave
To fate his own. He meets his doom:
Unknown shall soon us all consume.

Tenebrous shadows closer come:
To others fear, a feast to some.
Fear not the dark, rely on hope -
It is the only way to cope
With what has happened to this land,
Now tiny speck, once empire grand. 

2017-11-21, Midnight
